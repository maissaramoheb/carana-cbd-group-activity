Stage 1 · BOPPPS Bridge-In & Methodology

Monitoring, Mentoring & Advising (MMA) Operational Strategy

Estimated time: 30 minutes

Implementation Realities & Resilience

Per Lesson 4 of the UNPOL JST curriculum, "The implementation of any plan will face obstacles and unintended setbacks. CBD actors must integrate an attitude of resilience into their work and develop problem-solving mechanisms to overcome these situations."

  • Co-location is essential: MMA is most effective when advisers share daily working spaces with counterparts.
  • Avoid doing the work yourself: Substitution erodes local ownership and creates dependency.
  • Patience and emotional intelligence: Sustainable reform takes time in post-conflict transitions.

The MMA Triad (UNPOL MMA Manual 2017.14)

  • Monitoring: Observing daily police performance, data intake, and human rights compliance to retain a clear operational picture without interfering.
  • Advising: Providing situational assistance, technical analysis, and strategic guidance to senior command on organizational and policy matters.
  • Mentoring: Guiding individual professional development, coaching personal problem-solving skills, and inspiring ethical conduct through role modeling.
