### Project Overview & Educational Purpose

**CARANA CBD Learning Lab** is an interactive, local-first simulation environment supporting the six phases of United Nations Police (UNPOL) Capacity-Building and Development (CBD). 

Built specifically for UN peacekeeping pre-deployment training, international police development advisers, and security sector reform (SSR) specialists, the software guides learning syndicates through the complete lifecycle of mission planning—from initial situational scanning to sustainable transition and operational handover.

This maintenance release (`v1.0.1`) completes the formal research software publication and archival workflow with Zenodo, registering permanent citation metadata and digital object identifier (DOI) preservation.

---

### Core Software Capabilities

- **Six Interconnected Learning Modules (36 Interactive Stages):**
  - **Module 1 (Situational Analysis):** PESTEL-S conflict scan, actor response analysis, 3 CBD perspectives, 4-Quadrant Stakeholder Matrix, official 5 Areas × 6 Dimensions Matrix (78 intersections), 2 × 2 SWOT matrix, and baseline indicators.
  - **Module 2 (Objective Setting & Prioritisation):** UN mandate alignment (SSR & SDG 16), official 6-category prioritisation chart, SMART objective formulation engine, and KPI performance framework.
  - **Module 3 (Planning Activities):** Theory of Change causal pathways, 4 × 4 Logframe Matrix with hierarchical numbering, Results-Based Budgeting (RBB), 3 × 3 Risk Matrix with High/Medium/Low zone calculations, and contingency plans.
  - **Module 4 (Implementation & MMA):** Monitoring, Mentoring and Advising (MMA) field practice logs, Counterpart Role Reversal simulation, dynamic problem-solving via the "5 Whys" root-cause methodology, and activity tracking.
  - **Module 5 (Evaluation & Adjustment):** Mid-term review against Module 1 baselines, simulated 8-Month Crisis scenario, KPI variance analysis (supporting legitimate "Exceeded" progress), and UNPOL 3-Tier Adjustment Decision Matrix.
  - **Module 6 (Transition & Handover):** Transition definition distinguishing CBD activity handover from full mission drawdown, application of the Four Key Principles (Early Planning, UN Integration, Local Ownership, Communication), 3-phase Handover Roadmap, doctrine gazetting, academy curriculum integration, gender-responsive budgeting, and formal Handover Protocol Instrument with designated signatories.
- **Reporting & Dossier Engine:** Print-optimized 6-phase master mission dossier with dynamic verification watermarks and clean SVG matrix visualizers.
- **Local-First Architecture & Concurrency:** Zero remote server dependency, client-side `localStorage` persistence, stable stakeholder IDs (`sh-1`..`sh-8`) with record-level multi-tab reconciliation, and non-destructive corrupted data quarantine.
- **Session Safety:** Safe "Start New Session / Reset Lab" modal with automatic pre-wipe JSON download and cross-tab broadcast synchronization token.
