## 2. Identification of Passive, Repetitive, or Underdeveloped Screens (`E10`, `E11`)

Across the 38 screens in Lessons 2–6, **21 screens have no `.applied-lab`** and rely solely on clicking 3–4 `.visual` definition buttons followed by a 3-option `.decision` MCQ:
- **Lesson 2 (4 of 8 screens without lab):** `02-01` (System alignment), `02-02` (Ownership), `02-07` (Counterpart briefing — has worked contrast), `02-06` (Handoff).
- **Lesson 3 (5 of 10 screens without lab):** `03-01` (Results chain), `03-03` (Results-based budgeting — has worked contrast), `03-04` (Census & vetting), `03-09` (Policy route — has worked contrast), `03-05` (QIPs & trust funds).
- **Lesson 4 (5 of 7 screens without lab):** `04-01` (Local fit), `04-04` (Monitor implementation), `04-05` (Problem-solving / SARA), `04-07` (New evidence — has worked contrast), `04-06` (Counterpart perspective).
- **Lesson 5 (3 of 6 screens without lab):** `05-01` (Ask what changed), `05-02` (Credibility & evaluation roles), `05-05` (Evaluation practice handoff).
- **Lesson 6 (4 of 7 screens without lab):** `06-01` (Activity transition scope), `06-03` (Four principles), `06-04` (Authority vs coordination), `06-06` (Transition handoff).

### Why This Matters Pedagogically
Not every screen needs a text builder—adding builders to all 38 screens would cause severe cognitive fatigue. However, on screens without a lab, the `.visual` block (`Explore the relationship`) currently uses the exact same 3–4 text-button reveal mechanism (`data-node`), while on the 17 screens *with* a lab, the learner must complete **both** the `.visual` button clicks, the `.applied-lab`, **and** a `.decision` MCQ on a single tall scrolling page (`E10`).

### Proposed Three-Pattern Screen Rhythm (`E10`)
Instead of one monolithic template across all 38 screens, differentiate screens into three explicit cognitive patterns:
1. **Pattern A — Explain & Compare (15 screens):** Lead with a true relational visual or worked contrast (`before/after`), concise core points, and a sharp applied decision. Omit redundant headings.
2. **Pattern B — Investigate & Classify (10 screens):** Lead with a dilemma or evidence set (`classify`, `priority`, `risk`, or interactive diagnostic loop), letting the learner test hypotheses before reading the takeaway; remove the duplicate bottom `.decision` MCQ where the classification tool already tests the exact same concept.
3. **Pattern C — Construct & Self-Review (13 screens):** Make the structured builder or workbench handoff the primary task on the screen, supported by an adjacent concept reference and field-level self-review rubric (`E06`, `E12`).

## 3. Deepening Critical Thinking, Applied Judgement & Transfer (`E06`, `E08`, `E09`, `E12`, `E13`, `E16`)

### A. From Completeness Checking to Transparent Reasoning Review (`E06`, `E12`)
- Currently, all 9 builder scaffolds (`02-04`, `02-08`, `03-10`, `03-07`, `04-02`, `05-03`, `05-04`, `06-05`, `06-07`) run the same check (`platform/learning-tools.js` line 31): if every field has `>= 1` non-whitespace character, `#lab-feedback` displays `"Your draft is ready for self-review, not automatically approved. " + x.review`.
- **Immediate Enhancement (`E06`):** Rename the button to `"Open self-review prompts"`, use neutral styling, and attach a concrete 2–3 item self-check list to each field (e.g., on `03-10` Field 1 *Measure*: *"Does this measure observe change in routine use rather than counting launch attendance?"*) alongside the collapsible illustrative approach.
- **Transformative Enhancement (`E12`):** Add an explicit self/peer/facilitator rubric where the learner highlights which phrase in their draft satisfies each criterion, records one unresolved uncertainty or assumption, and can compare their first draft with a revised draft after reading the illustrative example.

### B. Connecting the Thread Across Lessons 1–6 (`E08`, `E13`)
- Currently, Lessons 2–6 repeatedly reference an invented *"case-file review routine"* in prose, but each screen starts from a blank state. A learner's SMART objective in `02-04` is not visible when they write the indicator plan in `02-08`, the logframe row in `03-10`, the evaluation question in `05-03`, or the phased handover in `06-05`.
- **Connected Evidence-to-Decision Notebook (`E08`):** Allow the learner to carry one bounded thread across the six lessons—displaying their prior lesson's drafted objective/indicator/logframe row as a pinned context card at the top of the next lesson's builder so they experience how a vague objective in Lesson 2 makes logframe verification in Lesson 3 and evaluation in Lesson 5 harder.
- **Evolving Professional Case (`E13`):** Introduce two bounded mid-course evidence updates (e.g., in Lesson 4 `04-07` a shift-rota conflict emerges; in Lesson 5 `05-06` overall file completion rises while one shift faces a new barrier) that invite the learner to revise their earlier plan and justify the adjustment.

### C. Bridging Self-Paced Lessons to the Original CARANA Workbenches (`E09`, `E07`)
- At the end of each lesson, generate a **Readable Study & Handoff Sheet (`E07`, `E09`)** that pairs what the learner practised in the lesson's invented teaching case with the exact stages and fields of the corresponding CARANA workbench (`module1.html`–`module6.html`), plus an optional, user-consented local copy of non-conflicting draft notes.

### D. Retrieval and Justification in Checkpoints (`E16`)
- Across all 78 checkpoint questions (36 in Lesson 1; 42 in Lessons 2–6), Run 02 significantly improved distractor plausibility and added `optionFeedback` in Lessons 2–6 (`RUN02_ASSESSMENT_REVIEW.md`).
- However, Lesson 1's 36 checkpoint questions (`course/lesson1-data.js`) still use question-level `why` feedback rather than option-specific distractor feedback, and neither shell asks the learner to identify *which condition failed* before retrying a missed question (`E16`). Adding option-specific feedback parity and a lightweight retrieval/justification step on retry will prevent mechanical answer-clicking.
