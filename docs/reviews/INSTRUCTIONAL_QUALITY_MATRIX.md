C1–C6 denote the six checkpoints. All 36 items were read against their local learning purpose and answer feedback. Scope gaps against p. 2: MCQs can check recognition and prioritisation; they cannot demonstrate changes to interpersonal practice, in-country comparison, or competence. The classroom joint analysis/output and facilitated critique are not reproduced by guide completion. For facilitated use, review one cell, one evidenced SWOT entry and one baseline observation together; ask what was uncertain, what source could change the judgement, and who may be affected. Do not distribute the expected outcome with the learner package.
