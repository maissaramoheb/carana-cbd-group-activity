## Protected content and incomplete validation

Expected solutions excluded from new learner content: L2 p.24; L3 pp.23–29; L4 p.13; L5 p.11; L6 p.14. Only teaching and participant instructions support the authored digital activities. Full PDFs remain outside the public artifact.

All 38 screens have a trainer explanation and applied decision; 17 screens add construction/classification/parameter exploration, and five use explicit worked contrasts. Human reviewers must judge accuracy, completeness, cognitive load, assessment discrimination and work-product quality. Pilot results and official curriculum/rights acceptance remain absent.
