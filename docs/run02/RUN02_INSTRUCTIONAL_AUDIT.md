# Run02 instructional audit

Authority: actual 2023 CBD lesson plans recovered from the verified Run01 backup, cross-referenced with the source hashes in docs/expansion/sources.json. These are the available editions; no assertion is made that they replace current mission guidance. Teaching, objectives, participant instructions and facilitation expectations were read separately from protected expected outcomes.

## Findings from Run01

| Lesson | Evidence of insufficient depth | Instructional consequence | Run02 treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 Objective setting | Six screens covered strategic fit, ownership, prioritisation, SMART and indicators, but required little construction. Teaching pp. 14–21 and participant task p. 22/pp. 29–30 require comparison, objective formulation and measurement reasoning. | Recognition of a good answer can be mistaken for ability to formulate one. Weighting, prerequisites and the interpretation of apparently precise measures remained implicit. | Eight-screen benchmark: weighting sensitivity, SMART drafting, objective briefing contrast, evidence classification and an indicator plan. Maintain actual participant chart and workbench unchanged. |
| 3 Planning | One screen compressed specialist tools from pp. 12–16; logframe columns were described but not related through a learner-created row (pp. 5–7). Risk was a description rather than a response plan (pp. 18–20). | Learners could name a result chain without testing its mechanism, safeguards, evidence or contingency. | Separate professional tool purposes, construct a logframe row, test assumptions, explore likelihood/impact and draft a contingency response. |
| 4 Implementation | Seven Cs and problem-solving were recognisable in six screens, but there was no written communication repair or evidence-based adoption diagnosis. Teaching pp. 4–10 distinguishes delivery, change support, monitoring and iterative diagnosis. | Choosing a respectful sentence is weaker evidence than constructing one for a specific audience and checking its interpretation. | Revise a counterpart message, distinguish delivery/adoption evidence, and use a new perspective-change case before the original role-taking exercise. |
| 5 Evaluation | Five screens covered actors/principles and methodology (pp. 4–8), but little evidence interpretation or recommendation construction. Participant task p. 9 asks for evaluation, recommendations and planning consequences. | A favourable number could be accepted without attention to comparison, unequal effects or alternative explanations. | Add a worked comparison, classify claims, draft an evaluation design and explain a management response without supplying scenario solutions. |
| 6 Transition | Six screens correctly limited scope to an activity but compressed integrated assessment, readiness, rights/gender, resources and dialogue (pp. 4–11). Paired task p. 12/handout p. 17 cannot be reproduced by saving an individual form. | A plan can look complete without agreement about continuity or evidence of capacity. | Distinguish triggers, explore readiness constraints, draft phased responsibilities and rehearse a difficult handover conversation. Keep authorised decisions distinct from learner practice. |

## Cross-cutting findings

- Every Run01 concept used similar reveal buttons. Useful definitions were repeatedly followed by strongly signposted MCQs. Run02 retains those foundations where helpful but adds different reasoning tasks: classify evidence, vary assumptions, write a draft and examine a worked contrast.
- Many checkpoint alternatives were implausible or expressed as obviously unacceptable actions. Revise them into plausible but incomplete courses of action, with option-specific explanations. All-correct, unlimited-retry study rules remain unchanged.
- Some Lesson2/3 transcripts repeated the displayed material almost verbatim. Rewrite them around interpretation, caveats and an adviser’s reasoning; keep text alternatives and human narration review gates.
- Expanded content requires a new version: retain previous checkpoint records in export, restart current-version study rather than implying old assessment completion validates new material. Preserve browser-local work and make limitations explicit.
- Artwork briefs need teaching questions, composition, style references, alt text and publication constraints. Specifications are not produced imagery. No paid generation is authorised.
