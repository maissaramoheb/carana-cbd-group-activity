## Behaviour and compatibility

Unanswered items require a choice; incorrect items retain choices and provide specific feedback. Reviewing a screen does not discard the current tab’s checkpoint answers. Passing a checkpoint unlocks the next section. A reload now returns to the checkpoint view when that was the saved view; partial answers are intentionally not persisted across reload.

Run02 contentVersion restarts checkpoint completion for revised material. Earlier Run01 checkpoint records are retained in previousProgress in the export rather than silently converted to mastery of changed material. Draft fields are bounded and local; unexpected keys/values are dropped when loaded. Legacy Lesson1/lab keys and assessment rules are untouched.

Classifications and parameter tools remain optional formative practice. Drafts receive completeness and reflective prompts, never an essay score, professional approval or a certification claim. A high weighted score does not override constraints; the risk product is an explicitly illustrative aid.

## Educational limits

Better distractors and more active tasks are design improvements, not measured discrimination or learning gains. Subject experts must still examine ambiguity, realistic difficulty and the quality of learner justification. Screen completion cannot demonstrate communication, contextual policing judgement, facilitated group work or sustained workplace performance. No field pilot was initiated.
