# Additional CBD lessons: source-based content review

Reviewed 18 November 2026 against the actual local July 2023 teaching and participant PDFs. This is a source-based editorial review, not independent UN curriculum approval or a field-validation result. Lessons 2–3 were reviewed by an agent who did not author those files; Lessons 4–6 were self-reviewed by their author. All findings should remain visible to the owner and expert reviewer.

## Review result

No clear doctrinal contradiction or imported facilitator model answer was identified in the reviewed additional-lesson data. Thirty teaching screens across Lessons 2–6 provide a compact first digital adaptation, with original applied examples, reveal interactions, transcripts, decisions and checkpoint questions. These authored explanations should not be described as the complete official course or proof of practical competence.

The strongest fidelity decisions are the explicit activity-level scope of Transition, separation of output from outcome, continued national ownership, rights/due-process safeguards for vetting, and a clear boundary between independent workbench preparation and facilitated performance assessment. New cinematic entries are specifications, not delivered illustrations or generated media.

The blank chart includes more than the four teaching lenses, including donor interest and weights. | Add the exact participant task constraints to the handoff and distinguish teaching lenses from the complete scoring chart. | CONTENT-02 | P2 | L3 03-01 gives the result hierarchy and mentions measures/evidence/external factors, but learners do not yet explore how all four logframe columns relate on the same row. Source p. 7 specifically recommends working through columns visually. | Add a concise row-oriented explanation (result, verification measure, source, assumption/external factor), or explicitly retain construction as the linked practical task. ## Facilitator-material check

Reviewed against teaching and participant pages while intentionally excluding expected-outcome responses: L2 p. 24, L3 pp. 23–29, L4 p. 13, L5 p. 11 and L6 p. 14. No scenario-specific contents from those keys were identified in the reviewed learner JSON. Recommending reflection, rights sensitivity or local ownership is ordinary teaching content, not disclosure of a model answer. Full source PDFs and the private source inventory must remain outside public build output.

The original activities and any scenario answers they already contain require their own preservation and distribution review. This review does not claim that unchanged legacy modules were redesigned, fully audited or made appropriate for high-stakes assessment.

## Acceptance boundary

Content is suitable for a controlled review preview with clear draft and media status. Expert validation, actual work-product review, human listening review once new audio is produced, physical-device/assistive-technology checks and any official adoption remain open. The Phase 1 field pilot stays deferred; no learner results were collected.

## Corrections implemented following review

CONTENT-01–03 were addressed before final verification: 02-06 now names the 6–10 objective task, excluded risk category and donor-interest distinction; 03-01 explains the four logframe columns and clearly leaves construction to the workbench; 03-04 adds expertise, interpersonal skills, adviser limits and co-location. Transcript copy was updated for the same screens. CONTENT-04–06 remain explicit release limitations.
