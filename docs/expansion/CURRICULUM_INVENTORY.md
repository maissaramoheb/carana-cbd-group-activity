## Assessment and performance requirements

CBD outline p. 12 specifies knowledge pre/post assessment, pre/post self-confidence survey (recommended follow-up after six months), skills assessment after each lesson, continuous attitude/motivation observation and course critique. Supporting Evaluation resources hold instruments; these were not independently inventoried here. No numeric pass threshold is established by this inventory. Digital authored checkpoints are formative adaptation, not the official qualification.

The connected CBD products are situational analysis → SMART/prioritised objectives → logframe/risk → counterpart-informed problem-solving → evaluation/adjustment → transition. Dialogue, observed performance and facilitated reflection cannot be replaced by screen completion.

MMA Course Introduction pp. 13–14 specifies knowledge pre/post assessment, scenario-role-play skills assessment, course critique, readiness survey and facilitator/peer values/behaviour feedback. COP Course Introduction pp. 15–16 specifies knowledge pre/post tests, lesson knowledge/skills tools, continuous attitude/motivation observation, critique and readiness survey. No official pass percentage is inferred.

## Discrepancies and open gates

- CBD L2 plan uses singular Performance Framework; the outline uses plural Frameworks. Preserve exact citation title and one clear UI display label.
- CBD L6 is Transition. P. 4 restricts it to CBD activity transition, not full mission drawdown.
- CBD L5 p. 9 gives total exercise 2:15, but text components 10 + 1:45 + 30 minutes total 2:25; embedded slide gives 10 + 1:35 + 30 = 2:15. Retain declared total; detailed facilitation timing needs SME resolution.
- COP outline cover development v1.0/2023 contrasts with 2021 footers; L4 includes a 2020 footer. Do not resolve document lineage using filenames alone.
- COP Introduction p. 10 calls L1 Implementation of COP, unlike actual L1 plan and outline Introduction to Community-Oriented Policing. Use actual L1 title.
- MMA/COP introductions use the string 1.45 hours; retain this notation until intended minutes are confirmed rather than calculating 1.45 decimal hours.
- Expected-outcome answers are not confined to CBD Annex D: protect L2 p. 24, L3 pp. 23–29, L4 p. 13, L5 p. 11 and L6 p. 14 and associated facilitator notes. Do not expose role-specific MMA scenario information to all learners.
