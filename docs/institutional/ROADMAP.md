# Sponsor implementation roadmap

This is a proposed delivery sequence, not a funded schedule. The field pilot remains deferred. Estimates assume timely access to designated reviewers; actual dates follow source reconciliation, scope and rights decisions.

| Stage | Work and evidence | Accountable decision | Indicative elapsed window |
|---|---|---|---|
| Foundation | Verified source, release manifest, protected exercises, reproducible media/build and recoverable backup | Owner accepts technical baseline and source reconciliation | 1–2 weeks |
| CBD increments | Lesson-by-lesson maps, explanations, conceptual visuals, transcripts, practice, checkpoints, activity handoffs and tests | Curriculum authority approves each adaptation; owner accepts release | 8–12 weeks after scope agreement, with parallel expert review |
| Media and accessibility | Approved narration/artwork specifications, human read-along, keyboard/screen-reader and real-device checks | Media/rights reviewers and accessibility reviewer accept evidence | Continuous, with 2–4 weeks reserved before adoption |
| Deferred learner validation | Recruit representative practitioners only after owner authorisation; collect proportionate usability and applied-output evidence | Owner authorises pilot; designated evaluator reports without certifying competence | Later decision; allow 4–6 weeks including revision |
| MMA/COP examples | Confirm source boundaries, convert one agreed example per curriculum, test shared infrastructure | Curriculum owners accept subject-specific maps | Additional 4–8 weeks under Option B |
| Operations | Named steward, support route, update cadence, archived releases and funded annual maintenance | Institutional partner accepts service responsibilities | First year under agreed service levels |

Do not compress doctrinal review, rights clearance or accessibility acceptance to meet a promotional date. Proposed checkpoints are reviewable lesson increments, not a promise that a fixed screen count completes a curriculum. Release additional CBD work on isolated previews; incomplete media and human validation must stay visible in release status.
