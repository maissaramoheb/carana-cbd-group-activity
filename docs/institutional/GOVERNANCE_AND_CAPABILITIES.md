# Governance and product capabilities

## Responsibilities

| Decision | Proposed responsible party | Evidence required |
|---|---|---|
| Institutional adoption and permitted use | Designated institutional sponsor | Recorded scope, intended context and authority |
| Curriculum correctness | Authorised subject-matter/curriculum reviewer | Source-to-objective-to-mechanism-to-assessment map; documented decisions |
| IP, branding and media rights | Rights holder or designated legal/permissions authority | Permission terms and attribution requirements |
| Product direction and production release | Platform owner | Preview acceptance, known limitations and rollback point |
| Build, security, reliability and backup | Engineering maintainer | Executable checks, media integrity, release manifest, restoration test |
| Accessibility and learner inclusion | Qualified reviewer with representative users | Automated and manual evidence; remediation decisions |
| Future learner validation | Designated evaluator | Approved protocol, consent, data plan and findings |

Role assignments are proposals; no institutional role or commitment has been confirmed. Software authorship does not confer curriculum-approval authority.

## Capability inventory

Verified working Lesson 1 baseline: six instructional sections and 18 screens; 18 existing AI narration MP3s and readable transcripts; 36 authored checkpoint items; interactive concepts including PESTEL-S, SWOT, PIN, stakeholder influence/interest, triangulation and a 5×6 matrix; three cinematic scenes; formative choices and feedback; browser-local progress/export; same-origin access to original CARANA activities; dependency-free static build with joint script/audio hashes; versioned local backup and restore evidence.

In development: remaining CBD self-paced lessons, reusable lesson architecture and umbrella catalog. Each new lesson requires its own content and acceptance evidence. Planned only: full MMA/COP course conversion, institutional pilot validation, multilingual courses and any institutional LMS integration. Compulsory accounts, analytics and certification are not part of this stage.

Local progress is editable browser data. It is not an identity-verified record, cannot be used for high-stakes selection and may be lost when storage is cleared. Original workbench participant confirmation is self-report. The static platform is designed for individual use and facilitated discussion; it has no validated multi-user collaboration or institutional records system.
