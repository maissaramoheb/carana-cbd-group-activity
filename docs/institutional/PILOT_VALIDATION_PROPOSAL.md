# Deferred pilot validation proposal

**Status: proposal only. The owner has deferred Phase 1. No recruitment, field observation or participant-data collection is authorised or performed by this document.**

A later small formative study could test whether intended practitioners can navigate the course, interpret analytical tools, produce defensible practical work and use accessible alternatives. It should answer a bounded improvement question rather than attempt to prove professional competence.

Proposed participants: 12–18 representative police practitioners/trainers, selected to vary relevant experience, device and access needs. This is a planning assumption, not a completed sample or statistically powered effectiveness trial. Agree inclusion, voluntary participation and institutional permissions before recruitment.

Proposed sequence: (1) short ungraded baseline reasoning task; (2) individual course use with optional think-aloud observation; (3) equivalent applied scenario judgement and workbench output; (4) brief usability/accessibility interview; (5) optional follow-up transfer/reflection after an agreed interval. A curriculum expert should design equivalent tasks, avoid revealing facilitator answers and assess work with an explicit rubric. Use human review of evidence, reasoning and uncertainty; course scores alone are insufficient.

Measures: task completion and observed friction, navigation/recovery problems, interpretation of conceptual tools, quality of a sourced matrix/SWOT/baseline entry, accessibility barriers and perceived relevance. Report missing data, prior experience and alternative explanations. Avoid causal learning-effectiveness claims without a suitable design and comparator.

Data plan: collect the minimum consented information, use participant codes, keep recordings optional and separately authorised, restrict access, set a retention period and aggregate reporting. No new platform analytics are needed for the proposed formative study. Do not upload operational police information or participant work to external AI services.

Proposed acceptance criteria must be agreed before the study: no unresolved critical access/data-loss defect; usable transcript and keyboard paths; observed ability to explain core distinctions; expert review of practical reasoning; documented revisions for recurrent confusion. These are proposal criteria, not results. The owner decides whether to authorise the pilot and whether its evidence supports further release.
