# Rights and permissions checklist

This is an operational checklist for a qualified rights authority; it is not a legal opinion. Access to a training PDF does not by itself establish permission to publish an adaptation.

| Item | Current basis | Required action before institutional distribution |
|---|---|---|
| Official UNPOL lesson content | Locally accessible official training set; source attribution maintained | Confirm the right to adapt, distribute and host each curriculum and its scenarios |
| UN name, marks and implied endorsement | Independent-adaptation disclaimer; no approval claimed | Obtain any necessary branding authorisation; retain clear separation from endorsement |
| Facilitator materials and expected outcomes | Known source boundaries; learner content must exclude model answers | Review each lesson/annex and confirm public participant package before release |
| Original CARANA exercises | Existing repository and attribution; original operation preserved | Confirm software and source-content licence scope, including any commercial or sponsored use |
| Existing AI narration and artwork | Existing deployed assets preserved; no new paid generation | Confirm provenance, provider terms, permissions and required disclosure; human review of scripts/audio |
| New media | Specifications/scripts may be prepared | Approve costs and rights before generation or procurement; preserve provenance and releases |
| Fonts and third-party assets | Existing presentation uses optional web fonts with fallbacks | Retain licence notices; review external request/privacy implications |
| Screenshots and demonstration | Use genuine application views with synthetic course content | Exclude learner records, personal data and unapproved facilitator answers |
| Future pilot data | No pilot initiated | Approve consent, collection purpose, minimisation, access, retention and reporting terms |

Record reviewer, scope, date, decision, conditions and supporting permission document for every closure. A sponsor's funding agreement does not substitute for the curriculum rights holder's permission. Do not insert government seals, institutional logos, accreditation claims or endorsement language on the basis of a conversation alone.
