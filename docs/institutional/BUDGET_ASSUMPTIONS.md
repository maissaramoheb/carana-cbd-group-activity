# Budget assumptions and scenarios

The figures below are internal, bottom-up USD planning assumptions prepared 18 November 2026. They are not market rates, supplier quotations, sponsor commitments or approved budgets. Each option is a complete alternative; do not add A to B or B to C. No expenditure or paid generation is authorised by this model.

| Scenario | Direct estimate | Contingency 10% | Planning total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| A — CBD Completion | $70,800 | $7,080 | $77,880 |
| B — Multi-Curriculum Pilot | $109,400 | $10,940 | $120,340 |
| C — Institutional Digital Learning Capability | $184,000 | $18,400 | $202,400 |

Development covers source conversion, interactions and release work. Instructional design covers learning maps, scripts and feedback. Curriculum expertise covers source and doctrine review. The media allowance covers a separately approved package of assets and narration; no automatic generation follows from this estimate. Quality assurance includes regression and browser checks; accessibility has a distinct specialist allocation. Hosting/support is a placeholder of $125 monthly for 12 months, not a Vercel quotation. Maintenance covers planned updates and regression rechecks. Evaluation covers preparation and a later owner-authorised validation exercise; the field pilot remains deferred.

Each day is an assumed eight-hour working day. Rates are blended costing inputs: development $450/day, instructional design $400/day, curriculum expertise $500/day, QA $350/day, accessibility $500/day, maintenance and evaluation $400/day. They do not establish an individual's pay. Workstream allocations may be delivered by overlapping roles, but the same hour must not be charged twice. Schedule estimates include review lag separately; day totals do not equal elapsed delivery time.

Option A covers five additional CBD lessons. B adds one initial MMA and one initial COP conversion example with source review. C uses 12 lesson-equivalent units to estimate a first-year programme; official lesson structures differ, so a sponsor must approve the actual scope before contracting. Audio duration, artwork count, revisions, languages, accessibility requirements, procurement, concurrent learners and service levels can materially alter the estimates.

Excluded: taxes/withholding, international travel, translation/localisation beyond English, identity/LMS integration, accreditation, devices and institutional staffing outside allocated days. Rights costs are unpriced because permission terms are unknown. Agree a contingency ceiling and change-control process; conduct scope and procurement validation before accepting any budget. Compare supported user requirements and learning risks, not only cost per screen.

Reproducibility: budget-model.json is the calculation source; budget-scenarios.csv repeats category quantities and totals for review. A changed quantity/rate must update both outputs and the comparison above. All totals were recalculated from quantity × rate; contingency is rounded to the nearest dollar.
