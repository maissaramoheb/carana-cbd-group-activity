Instructional Authority & Training Safeguard. CARANA and UNAC are fictional training settings developed by the UN Department of Peace Operations (DPO). This learning lab operationalizes the official UNPOL Job-Specific Training (JST) on Capacity-Building and Development curriculum. Group outputs record participant learning discussions only.

The Six-Phase UNPOL CBD Learning Journey

Six-Phase Cycle In Progress

Each module systematically reuses outputs from previous phases, guiding advisers from initial evidence gathering to sustainable transition.

Planning Activities

Ready to Launch

Core Focus: "What to" Action Planning & Logframe.

Theory of Change causal chains, Logical Framework Approach (Logframe Matrix), Results-Based Budgeting (RBB), 3 × 3 Risk Matrix, and Contingency Planning.

Input Pipeline: Converts Module 2 SMART objectives into developmental outcomes.

Phase 4 · Working

Implementation

Ready to Launch

Core Focus: "How to" Execution & MMA.

Monitoring, Mentoring and Advising (MMA) execution, overcoming institutional setbacks, change management resilience, and inter-agency mediation.

Input Pipeline: Operationalizes Module 3 Logframe activities into field MMA tracking and counterpart problem-solving.

Phase 5 · Working

Evaluation & Adjustment

Ready to Launch

Core Focus: "Did we" Measuring Impact & Adapting.

Deming cycle (Check & Act), 8-month crisis intelligence ("Le Galasien" expose & Section Chief email), KPI variance analysis, and 3-tier adjustment decisions.

Input Pipeline: Evaluates Module 2 KPIs and Module 3/4 planned activities against 8-month field realities.
Launch Module 5 Workbench →
Phase 6 · Working

Transition & Handover

Ready to Launch

Core Focus: "Sustain & exit" Institutionalising Ownership.

Tactical transition planning, the Four Key Principles (Early Planning, UN Integration, Local Ownership, Communication), institutionalising sustainable policing practice, and formal Handover Instrument.

Input Pipeline: Operationalizes successful Module 1–5 achievements into enduring national ownership and UNCT compact.
