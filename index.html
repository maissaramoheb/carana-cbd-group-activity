Instructional Authority & Training Safeguard. CARANA and UNAC are fictional training settings developed by the UN Department of Peace Operations (DPO). This learning lab operationalizes the official UNPOL Job-Specific Training (JST) on Capacity-Building and Development curriculum. Group outputs record participant learning discussions only.

The Six-Phase UNPOL CBD Learning Journey

Each module systematically reuses outputs from previous phases, guiding advisers from initial evidence gathering to sustainable transition.

Phase 1 · Working

Situational Analysis

Ready to Launch

Core Focus: "As is" Baseline & Conflict Scanning.

Conduct PESTEL-S conflict analysis, response mapping of external actors, 4-quadrant stakeholder influence mapping, the official 5 × 6 Areas–Dimensions Matrix, 2 × 2 SWOT analysis, and qualitative/quantitative baseline indicators.

Curriculum Alignment: Lesson 1 (Galasi CIS case, 57 paragraphs).
Launch Module 1 Workbench →
Phase 2 · Working

Objective Setting & Prioritisation

Ready to Launch

Core Focus: "To be" Desired End-State & SMART Goals.

Score and rank competing reform initiatives using the official 6-category prioritisation chart. Formulate SMART objectives and establish the quantitative/qualitative KPI framework.

Input Pipeline: Directly consumes Module 1 SWOT opportunities and baseline gaps.
Launch Module 2 Workbench →
Phase 3 · Planned

Planning Activities

Phase 3

Core Focus: "What to" Action Planning & Logframe.

Theory of Change causal chains, Logical Framework Approach (Logframe Matrix), Results-Based Budgeting (RBB), 3 × 3 Risk Matrix, and Contingency Planning.

Input Pipeline: Converts Module 2 SMART objectives into developmental outcomes.
Phase 4 · Planned

Implementation

Phase 4

Core Focus: "How to" Execution & MMA.

Monitoring, Mentoring and Advising (MMA) execution, overcoming institutional setbacks, change management resilience, and inter-agency mediation.

Phase 5 · Planned

Evaluation & Adjustment

Phase 5

Core Focus: "Did we" Measuring Impact & Adapting.

Evaluate local policing against the Module 1 baseline, analyze indicator variance, conduct self-reflection, and calibrate ongoing interventions.

Phase 6 · Planned

Transition & Handover

Phase 6

Core Focus: "Sustain & exit" Institutionalising Ownership.

Transition CBD programs to host-State authorities or succeeding development entities (UNCT, bilateral partners) with sustained local ownership.
