Why Objective Setting & Prioritisation Matter

Experience in post-conflict settings proves that there will always be more work to do than resources available to do it. CBD advisers will constantly be confronted with competing demands. To succeed, advisers must establish a mechanism for comparing the strategic value, implementability, risk, and complementarity of alternatives in an accountable, transparent, and defensible manner.

  • Align with UNSCR 2151 (Security Sector Reform) and UN SDG 16 targets.
  • Balance host-State perceived needs with international human rights standards.
  • Avoid endless reviews: establish transparent priorities and take action.

Key Principles of Sustainable Reform (Lesson 2 Slide 13)

  • Local (National) Ownership: Consistency with host-nation political objectives, leadership buy-in, and genuine counterpart participation at all stages.
  • Security-Development Nexus: Police CBD creates sustainable security; stable security allows long-term institutional reform.
  • Results-Orientation: Clear focus on outcomes (what changes for the community) rather than merely scheduled activities.
  • People-Centred Policing: Human security and protection of vulnerable groups over narrow regime security.
Curriculum Authority Note: In accordance with UNPOL JST Lesson 2 (Slide 11), police capacity-building must not be viewed as an isolated technical process. Objectives must reflect alignment with the broader justice sector (prosecutions, courts, corrections) and national oversight institutions.

Stage 3 · Activity 2.1 Step 2

UNPOL CBD Objective Prioritisation Chart (Lesson 2 p. 24 & 30)

Scoring Scale: 1 = Low, 2 = Medium, 3 = High
Official Scoring Model Rules (Lesson 2 p. 25 & 32):
  • 6 Strategic Categories (Policing, Environmental, Conflict Prevention, Human Rights, Gender, CPOC): each rated 1–3, weighted with factor of 2.00.
  • Need (Beneficiary Assessment), Implementability, and Donor Interest: rated 1–3, weighted at 1.00.
  • Risk: Inverse scale where 3 = Low Risk and 1 = High Risk (Weight: 1.00).
  • Complementarity: Weighted at 0.50 so it does not skew the overall balance.
