Stage 1 · 5 minutes

Start / Team details

Identify your group, read the case, and agree on a note-taker.

CARANA scenario reference

You are UNPOL CBD advisers supporting the Galasi Criminal Investigations Services (CIS) within the Carana National Police (CNP). The CNP faces shortages of qualified personnel, logistics, finance and training; corruption and weak public trust are serious concerns. The Galasi CIS case also identifies fragmented records, weak complaint follow-up, limited forensic capability, gaps in police-community information flow, and inconsistent policies and procedures.

Your task: discuss the evidence, identify the actors who matter, and propose a focused CBD response across individual, organisational and enabling-environment levels.

Source and adaptation note

Scenario facts are drawn from the supplied Carana Activity Workbook - Participant, especially the UNAC and police context (pp. 2, 5-6) and Activity 4, Galasi CIS (pp. 14-18). The six-stage digital workflow, shortened prompts, time allocations, review screen and export format are exercise-specific adaptations created for this session.
