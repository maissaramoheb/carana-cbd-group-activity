The 6 Ps of Police Planning

"Prior Preparation and Planning reduces the Possibility of Poor Performance!"

Planning is an essential managerial responsibility. In CBD, starting from activities (bottom-up) risks dictating goals by what is comfortable or familiar. In contrast, results-oriented planning must be top-down: Goals determine outcomes, outcomes dictate required outputs, and outputs define specific activities.

The 4 CBD Operational Tools (Lesson 3 Slide 11)

  • Census & Vetting: Identifying and defining the body of personnel; excluding human rights violators.
  • Training: Closing competence gaps, tailored to adult learning and host-nation culture.
  • Monitoring, Mentoring & Advising (MMA): Gaining situational picture, providing technical advice, guiding personal professional growth.
  • Policy Development: Establishing legal standard operating procedures (SOPs) and normative frameworks.

Stage 3 · Activity 3.1 Step 2

Risk Analysis Register & Mitigation Strategies

🎯 Stage Purpose

Identify operational, political, and cultural risks threatening CBD activity delivery.

📦 Expected Outputs

Comprehensive risk registry with likelihood, severity ratings, and mitigation actions.

⏭️ What Happens Next

Advance to Stage 4 to plot identified risks onto the official UNPOL 3 × 3 Risk Matrix.

Per UNPOL CBD Lesson 3 (Slide 17 & 19), Risk is defined as the likelihood of a threat occurring. Assign scores for Likelihood (1=Low, 2=Med, 3=High) and Potential Impact (1=Low, 2=Med, 3=High). Formulate proactive mitigation strategies to reduce likelihood and contingency plans to decrease impact.
