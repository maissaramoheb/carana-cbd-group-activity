# CARANA CBD E-Learning — Lesson 1 Illustrated Learning Direction
**Version:** 0.1 editorial proposal, 17 November 2026
**Status:** Three concept samples generated for review; no production-website changes authorised by this document.

## Instructional source
- **Primary lesson:** *Job-Specific Training for UN Police 2021 — CBD Lesson 1: Situational Analysis* (lesson plan PDF, July 2023 compilation).
- Chapter sequencing and terminology must follow the course source: conflict analysis, response analysis, CBD analysis, stakeholder analysis, five CBD analysis areas, six cross-cutting dimensions, baseline, CARANA practice.
- The digital course is an independent instructional adaptation; **do not imply official UN endorsement**. No invented UN/UNPOL insignia, fake national flags, or fabricated CARANA findings.
- Do not reproduce facilitator-only Annex D assessment solutions in art.

## Art direction — candidate baseline
Standalone, landscape **16:9 story illustrations**, *not* screenshots or proposed replacements for the approved course UI. Elevated editorial graphic-novel artwork suitable for adult police/peace-operations professionals: intentional composition, dignified expressive characters, detailed but uncluttered institutional context, cinematic daylight, restrained ink lines and sophisticated navy–teal–mint–ochre tones.

Use consistent recurring people: female civilian CBD adviser, dark hair secured back, navy shirt and muted olive vest; local police counterpart in modest light blue shirt; broader cast drawn respectfully without simplistic stereotypes. No hero-combat imagery, guns, violent confrontation, exaggerated facial expressions or childish superhero tropes. Static artwork should be legally and culturally neutral.

Never rely on generated lettering to explain a technique. **All titles and analytical explanations remain accessible HTML text** outside the image, with accurate alt-text and an authored one-line figure caption. Use only source-supported claims; examples may be explicitly illustrative.

## Integration acceptance criteria
- Preserve approved homepage, three-column lesson workspace, typography, narration and all six six-question assessment gates.
- Illustrations add meaning, not duplication. Prefer one landscape media card per selected screen, with a one-sentence caption and concise alt-text.
- **Responsive:** scale proportionally, stay inside the reading column, offer mobile-appropriate display; never crowd a screen's interactive control or audio player.
- **Performance:** deliver optimised modern image formats and sensible responsive sizes; lazy-load images below the fold, reserve aspect ratio to prevent layout jumps.
- **Accessibility:** meaningful alt descriptions; no crucial information exclusively inside artwork; reduce motion unless genuine learner benefit.
- **Source fidelity:** original scenarios remain clearly identified as fictional; no precise operational details or unsupported local country representation.
- **Publishing gate:** confirm one consistent art style and characters on three sample assets, then integrate only approved illustrations into isolated preview; test Safari/mobile and cross-section continuity before production promotion.
