# CARANA CBD E-Learning — Lesson 1 Gold Standard Blueprint (v0.5)

**Status:** Instructional preview implemented; natural English audio production NOT YET COMPLETE. Do not advertise as fully narrated.

## Curriculum basis and boundaries

Primary source: Job-Specific Training for United Nations Police (2021), CBD Lesson 1: Situational Analysis. Source file: JSTC_CBD_L01_LP_Situational Analysis_072023.pdf.

The official lesson links conflict, response and CBD analysis and stresses proper sequencing; subsequent topics cover stakeholder analysis, five institutional CBD areas, six cross-cutting dimensions, SWOT and baseline. Its original long facilitated exercise must not be misrepresented as equivalent to a short self-paced digital module.

The course retains six 6-of-6 knowledge checkpoints and original Module 1 workbench. Facilitator-only Annex D contains expected exercise outcomes and is excluded from learner materials.

## Natural voice production brief

**Character:** One clear international-English voice with the warmth, confidence and judgement of an experienced UNPOL training facilitator; measured, conversational, no excessive announcer energy or synthetic monotony.

**Production policy:** Use reviewed human narration or an explicitly approved, rights-cleared premium natural-voice service. Never substitute the browser's generic robotic text-to-speech without disclosure.

**Voice files:** Each lesson screen expects an independent track named 01-01.mp3 through 06-03.mp3 in the course/audio/en/lesson1 folder. Playback is controlled by course/lesson1-audio-manifest.js. That manifest is intentionally empty pending recordings.

**Editorial sequence:** Review all transcripts against the official source; verify pronunciation of CARANA, Galasi, UNPOL, CBD, PESTEL-S, PIN and CPOC; audition the voice on screens 01-01, 02-01 and 05-02; approve tone and pauses; record all 18 tracks; review each clip against visuals and transcripts; check no answers leak into checkpoints; then add final files to the release manifest.

**Learner controls after approval:** Optional play/pause, replay, adjustable speed, native audio failure handling, and transcript access. No autoplay by default. Without an approved recording the interface shows the transcript and explicitly indicates that audio is pending; it does not render fake or nonfunctional playback controls.

## Usability and educational controls

- Formative scenario choices explain why an option is defensible or premature. They never bypass the six mandatory knowledge checks.
- PESTEL-S and the 30-cell CBD matrix offer analytical prompts, NOT prefilled findings about CARANA.
- Learners can study without audio and without an account.
- Original CARANA workbench code, rights notices, participant self-confirmation and local storage are unchanged.
- Browser lesson progress remains under carana_cbd_course_v1; original activity data remains under carana_cbd_lab_v1.
